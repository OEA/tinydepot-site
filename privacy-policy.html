Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Data collected: none

Tiny Depot collects no personal data

Your game progress is saved on your device and nothing is sent to us. The app plays without an internet connection.

  • No accounts
  • No ads
  • No analytics
  • No tracking

This policy covers the Tiny Depot app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. “We” means the developer of Tiny Depot.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app never sends data off your device, so we receive no personal data from it.

Tiny Depot does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It does not track you across other apps or websites, and it does not use your device’s advertising identifier.

What’s saved on your device

To keep your place in the game, the app saves two small pieces of game data in its own storage:

Saved itemWhy it’s saved
Current levelSo the game reopens where you left off
Buses you’ve seenSo each new bus is introduced only once

This data is created as you play and is used only by the game. We can’t see it.

Device backups

If you back up your device, for example with iCloud Backup, a computer or Time Machine, the backup can include this game data along with your other app data. Apple or your backup provider handles those backups under its own terms. We have no access to them.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. No third party receives data from the app.

Apple runs the App Store and handles your download under its own privacy policy. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may show us summary statistics and crash reports. We use these only to fix problems and improve the game.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it. On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app removes it. On a Mac, moving the app to the Trash may leave this data in your user Library folder until you remove it. Copies in an older backup stay until that backup is replaced or deleted.

We hold no data from the app, so there is nothing for us to look up or delete, and there is no account to close. The app doesn’t ask for any permissions or consent, so there is nothing to revoke.

Children

Tiny Depot collects no personal data from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

If you email us

We receive your email address and anything you include in your message. We use it only to reply, and we delete it when you ask or once we no longer need it.

This web page

This page has no analytics or ads of its own. The service that hosts it may keep standard logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version at this address and update the date at the top.
