Clear the lot. Fill the buses.

Tiny Depot is a colour-matching bus puzzle for iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad and Mac. Tap a bus, park it in a bay, and watch the riders hop aboard.

See how it plays Coming soon

From gridlock to all aboard

  1. Tap a bus

    It drives the way its roof arrow points. If another bus is in the way, it bumps and rolls back.

  2. Park it in a bay

    With a clear path, the bus takes the first free bay. Bays are limited, so your order matters.

  3. Fill the seats

    Riders of the same colour hop aboard. A full bus leaves and frees its bay for the next one.

Five places to untangle

Each one has its own lot, kerb and bus stop, from quiet streets to neon nights.

Bigger buses, more seats

New buses roll in as you play, and each one takes a bigger crowd.

  • Classic

    3 seats, from level 1

  • Round Shuttle

    4 seats, from level 5

  • Double Decker

    5 seats, from level 12

  • Coach

    6 seats, from level 24

100 levels down one long road

Every level is a stop on the road through all five places. The lots get fuller as you go, and later levels close a bay. Each one is tested to make sure it can be solved.
